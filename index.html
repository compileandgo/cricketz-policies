Privacy Policy

Who we are

CricketZ ("we", "us", "our") operates a Facebook Page and connected Instagram account that publishes cricket news, facts, and stories. This policy explains how our software application (the "App") interacts with Meta's platforms (Facebook and Instagram) on our behalf.

What the App does

The App is a publishing tool. It automatically creates and publishes posts and Reels to the CricketZ Facebook Page and its connected Instagram Business account. The App does not collect, read, or process data from any other Facebook or Instagram user, account, or page. It does not access follower lists, direct messages, comments from other accounts, or any personal data belonging to third parties.

Information the App accesses

To publish content, the App uses the Meta Graph API with a limited set of permissions scoped only to the CricketZ Page and its connected Instagram account, specifically:

  • Permission to publish posts, images, and Reels to the CricketZ Page/Instagram account.
  • Basic Page/account identifiers (Page ID, Instagram Business Account ID) needed to direct publishing requests to the correct account.
  • An access token issued by Meta, used solely to authorize publishing actions.

The App does not access, store, or process personal data belonging to visitors, followers, or other users of the CricketZ Page or Instagram account.

How information is stored

The App stores the Meta access token and Page/account identifiers securely on its own server, solely to maintain the ability to publish scheduled content. No other personal data is collected or stored.

How information is used

The only use of any data accessed is to publish content (images, captions, Reels) to the CricketZ Page and Instagram account on a scheduled basis. We do not sell, share, rent, or use this data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the CricketZ Page.

Third-party sharing

We do not share any data obtained through the Meta Graph API with any third party. Data is not sold or used for purposes unrelated to publishing CricketZ content.

Data retention

Access tokens and account identifiers are retained only as long as the App is actively used to publish content to the CricketZ Page. If the App is discontinued or access is revoked, associated tokens are deleted.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to: jayantkushwaha.cs@gmail.com

Data Deletion Instructions

Because the App only publishes content to the CricketZ Page and Instagram account and does not collect personal data from other users, there is generally no third-party personal data held by the App to delete.

If you are the owner/admin of the connected CricketZ Facebook Page or Instagram account and would like any data associated with your account (such as stored access tokens) deleted from our systems, you can request this at any time:

  • Email jayantkushwaha.cs@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request".
  • Include the name of the Facebook Page or Instagram account associated with your request.
  • We will process deletion requests and remove any stored access tokens or account identifiers within 14 days, and confirm by email once complete.

You can also independently revoke the App's access at any time via Facebook Settings > Business Integrations or Instagram Settings > Apps and Websites, which immediately invalidates any access token the App holds.
